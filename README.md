# Royal Waves Store Annual Sales Report 2025

## Project Overview
This project delivers a comprehensive annual sales analysis and interactive Excel dashboard for **Royal Waves Store** for the year 2025. The core objective is to analyze historical sales data, track order performance, and uncover customer purchasing trends to help stakeholders strategize and drive sales growth for 2026.

## Dashboard Preview
![Royal Waves Store 2025 Dashboard](Dashboard/Screenshot%20\(80\).png)

## Key Features & Structure
* **Data Processing:** Cleaned and structured raw transactional dataset containing order details, customer demographics, sales amounts, order status, and regional fulfillment metrics.
* **Interactive Excel Dashboard:** Built with dynamic slicers for **Category**, **Channel**, and **Month** to filter insights across multiple metrics in real time.
* **Visualizations Included:**
  * Monthly Order vs. Sales Trends
  * Sales Breakdown by Gender (Men vs. Women)
  * Order Fulfillment Status (Delivered, Refunded, Returned, Cancelled)
  * Sales Contribution by Sales Channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nalli, Others)
  * Top 5 Performing States
  * Customer Segmentation by Age Group and Gender

## Key Insights & Findings
* **Gender Demographics:** Women account for the majority of purchases at approximately 65%, compared to men at 36%.
* **Geographic Performance:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh emerge as the top 3 revenue-generating states.
* **Age Group Impact:** The Adult age group (30–49 years) is the highest contributor, accounting for roughly 50% of total sales.
* **Sales Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra dominate as the primary high-volume sales channels.

## Final Recommendations for 2026 Growth Strategy
To optimize marketing spend and accelerate sales in 2026, Royal Waves Store should specifically target **women customers aged 30–49 years residing in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**, focusing promotional campaigns, targeted ads, and special coupons on high-performing channels like **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra**.
